# TileLang public call matrix

The pinned CUDA facade exports 717 distinct names. Each row links the declaration or binding used to inventory its parameters. The list includes double-underscore exports. It excludes Python builtins and local helpers, which have their own census rows. [TileLang facade](https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang/blob/35850c06875bcc84751417982bf7b4d662bafdbc/tilelang/language/__init__.py#L12) · [CUDA union](https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang/blob/35850c06875bcc84751417982bf7b4d662bafdbc/tilelang/cuda/language/__init__.py#L201) · [common union](https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang/blob/35850c06875bcc84751417982bf7b4d662bafdbc/tilelang/language/common.py#L329) · [pinned TVM parser](https://github.com/TileLang/tvm/blob/0215a96cba91eabac903afa4ec1e42c99257e4c3/python/tvm/tirx/script/parser/__init__.py#L37)

The keyword column inventories optional positional-or-keyword parameters, keyword-only parameters, variadic keywords, and keywords named by an admitted form. A dash denotes no such parameter identified on the cited binding. For operation wrappers, the column includes the explicitly handled `dtype` and the forwarded signature. The shape column gives the exact combinations allowed by the public TileLang acceptance predicate. Other keywords and combinations are outside that subset. A root number witnesses a call form inside that source; admission of the entire root is a separate check. A restricted call or value has **partial** coverage. **Unadmitted** exports are recognized by the surface grammar but have no admitted call. **Never** marks the explicit instruction and proxy-fence exclusions. These rows describe source syntax and admission. They make no lowering or runtime claim. [Subset and nine census roots](../TileLangSubset.md)
